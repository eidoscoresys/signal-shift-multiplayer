# SIGNAL SHIFT — Competition submission

**Title:** SIGNAL SHIFT — Keep the Signal Alive

**Play:** https://signal-shift.signal-shift.workers.dev

**Tagline:** Four roles. Three crises. One signal.

An orbital station is minutes from failure. Your crew must route its power, stabilize a heating reactor and execute a scrambled mechanical override. Every console holds a different piece of the solution. Talk, translate and coordinate: nobody can save the station alone.

SIGNAL SHIFT is a five-minute cooperative browser game for 2–8 players. Create a flight, share a six-character room code or invite, and join from individual phones or laptops. No accounts or installations. Power Engineer, Thermal Analyst, Signal Decoder and Systems Operator divide private clues and complementary controls. Small crews combine duties; larger crews share exclusive numbered sectors. Seeded puzzles vary every flight, while one shared integrity meter and server-controlled clock keep the mission urgent.

Built with React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind, backed by authoritative Cloudflare Durable Objects, SQLite persistence and WebSockets. Refresh/reconnect restores roles, sectors and mission progress. Use a voice call or sit together: spoken collaboration is the heart of the game.
